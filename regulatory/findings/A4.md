# A4 — Units and numeric formats silently misread

**Status:** PROPOSAL — UNSIGNED, pending clinical safety review (HD-E4).

## Hazardous situation
- `39,5` became 39.0 (hypothermia band shift).
- `<50` became 50.
- `120/80` became 120.
- °F values were never converted: 101.3 °F fell out of bounds and was lost.
- kPa was unhandled.
- An unrecognised unit silently took the type default.
- `/vitals` ignored the `unit` field entirely.

## Change (risk control, proposed)
- New `domain/services/unit_normaliser.py`, containing physical conversions only: °F→°C, and kPa→mmHg (×7.50062, BP only). Units incompatible with the type ⇒ rejected.
- HL7 adapter:
  - a decimal comma is read as a decimal;
  - an SN comparator or a compound/range value ⇒ `NON_EXACT_VALUE`, sample excluded (forces INCOMPLETE if it was a required input);
  - an unrecognised or incompatible OBX-6 ⇒ `AMBIGUOUS_UNIT`, sample excluded;
  - an absent OBX-6 keeps the documented per-type default.
- `/vitals`: the unit is honoured (converted); an incompatible unit ⇒ sample excluded with `AMBIGUOUS_UNIT`.

## Open
- An absent OBX-6 still defaults per type. Most fixtures and many real feeds omit it for NM vitals. A unitless temperature is assumed °C; a Fahrenheit value without a unit would then appear as a critical or out-of-range temperature and be flagged by A6, not silently accepted.
