# A1 — False NORMAL from missing or misread consciousness / O2 status

**Status:** PROPOSAL — UNSIGNED, pending clinical safety review (HD-E4).

## Hazardous situation
- A missing level of consciousness silently defaulted to Alert (0 points); a missing air/O2 observation defaulted to room air (0 points).
- Several real-world encodings were misread as "normal":
  - FiO2 as a fraction (0.4) and oxygen flow in L/min (2, 15) all parsed to room air;
  - "Drowsy" and CWE-coded AVPU (`V^Voice^L`) were dropped, and then Alert was assumed.
- The result could be a NEWS2 up to 5 points too low (consciousness 3 + O2 2), reported as a normal score.

## Change (risk control, proposed)
- `ScoringGate` requires both inputs. Absent ⇒ `MISSING_CONSCIOUSNESS` / `MISSING_O2_STATUS` ⇒ outcome `INCOMPLETE`, no score. `NEWS2Calculator` is unchanged; its internal defaults are no longer reachable through the orchestrator.
- The adapter reads O2 as follows:
  - flow with an L/min unit: > 0 ⇒ O2;
  - FiO2 percentage: > 21 % ⇒ O2;
  - FiO2 fraction: > 0.21 ⇒ O2;
  - a unitless value between 1 and 21 is `AMBIGUOUS_UNIT`, never room air.
- The adapter reads CWE AVPU (identifier, then text component). Unmapped values ⇒ `UNMAPPED_AVPU`.
- Adapter findings now reach the scoring outcome on both the `/ingest` and MLLP paths.

## Residual risk / open points
- The `NEWS2Calculator` docstring still says "six mandatory parameters" while enforcing five internally (protected file). This is corrected under R-06 in Phase 7. The effective behaviour is now governed by the gate (all seven required).
- The O2 unit vocabulary is limited to L/min, %, and unitless. Other units are rejected as ambiguous (fail-safe), not guessed.
