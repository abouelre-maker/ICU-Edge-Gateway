# A2 — Staleness, future and unparseable timestamps (includes N-03)

**Status:** PROPOSAL — UNSIGNED, pending clinical safety review (HD-E4).
Decisions Q1(b), Q3 and Q4 were made by delegation on 2026-09-29.

## Hazardous situation
- A two-day-old respiratory rate scored as current.
- Future timestamps were accepted.
- An unparseable or absent HL7 timestamp became `now()`, making corrupt data look fresh.
- The NEWS2 `effectiveDateTime` was the calculation time, not the observation time.

## Change (risk control, proposed)
- `ScoringGate` checks the most recent admitted sample of each of the 7 NEWS2 inputs against a per-parameter window:
  - older than the window ⇒ `STALE_OBSERVATION`;
  - beyond the future tolerance ⇒ `FUTURE_TIMESTAMP`.
  Either forces INCOMPLETE.
- **Windows are mandatory configuration** (`NEWS2_STALENESS_WINDOW_<TYPE>_SECONDS`). **No clinical default exists in `src/`.**
  - With any window unset, the gate withholds the score (`STALENESS_POLICY_UNSET`).
  - The service refuses to start (`config.validate_news2_scoring_config`, called in the lifespan).
  - The real values belong to a clinical reviewer (HD-E7).
- Future tolerance: `FUTURE_TIMESTAMP_TOLERANCE_SECONDS`, default 60 s. This is a clock-skew engineering parameter (Q3).
- Adapter: a present-but-unparseable OBX-14, or an unparseable MSH-7 used as fallback, ⇒ `UNPARSEABLE_TIMESTAMP` and the sample is skipped. Never `now()`.
- NEWS2 FHIR Observation: `effectiveDateTime` = latest input observation time; calculation time moved to `issued`.
- Test configuration: ten-year fixture windows are appended to `tests/conftest.py` (test-only; append-only per the directive).

## Open / residual
- **BLOCKED-TEST-CONFLICT A2-1** (HD-E6): when both OBX-14 and MSH-7 are absent, an interim keeps the sample with a receipt-time placeholder.
  - `UNPARSEABLE_TIMESTAMP` still forces INCOMPLETE (no score).
  - The per-vital Observation's effective time is the receipt time.
- Cross-message merging on MLLP is deferred (Q4): each fragment yields INCOMPLETE (known gap in `CLAIMS_CANONICAL.md`).
- The lenient helper `_parse_hl7_datetime` is kept unchanged for its existing tests, but is no longer used by the adapter.

## Verification
- `tests/unit/test_a2_staleness.py` (17 tests): fresh scored; two-day-old RR stale; stale ACVPU / O2; future beyond / within tolerance; unset policy; environment parsing (unset / valid / invalid); lifespan refuses to start; unparseable OBX-14; absent timestamps never scored; valid MSH-7 fallback; NEWS2 effective time = observation time.
- Full suite: 868 passed, 1 xfailed, 0 failed.
